# IronStore synthetic remote work policy
Effective 2026-01-01. Demonstration data only.

Employees may work remotely up to two days each week with their manager's approval.
Requests must be submitted in the People Portal at least one working day in advance.
Customer-facing store shifts require on-site attendance unless HR grants an exception.
This policy supersedes the 2023 remote work pilot. Contact HR if another policy conflicts.
